The Challenge

The Problem Participants Solved

01 / Problem Statement

Tourism & Hospitality in Akwa Ibom

Akwa Ibom — with its beautiful beaches, cultural festivals, cuisine, and emerging hospitality scene — has enormous untapped potential. Participants were challenged to use data to uncover what's holding it back and how to move forward.

02 / Objectives

What Teams Accomplished

  • Discovered trends & patterns in tourism data
  • Identified infrastructure and service gaps
  • Built dashboards & visualisations telling a clear story
  • Delivered strategic recommendations for Akwa Ibom

03 / Dataset

20 Years of Tourism Data

Teams received 20 years of simulated tourism data (2005–2025) with 500,000+ records including tourist arrivals, hotel occupancy, spending patterns, satisfaction scores, and reviews.

04 / Deliverables

What Teams Submitted

  • Cleaned Dataset — structured & analysis-ready
  • Dashboards — Power BI, Tableau, Python, R
  • 10-slide Presentation — insights & storytelling
  • Recommendations — data-backed strategic advice
Structure

Rules of Engagement

1

Teams

1–5 participants per team. Cross-functional skills encouraged.

2

Timeframe

192 hours (8 days) to complete the full submission.

3

Submission

Cleaned dataset/notebook + presentation slides + dashboards.

4

Tools Allowed

Python, R, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, and more.

Deliverables

Expected Outcomes

Each team delivered four key outputs by the end of the hackathon.

1

A Cleaned Dataset

Properly structured tourism dataset ready for analysis and visualisation.

2

Dashboards Revealing Key Insights

Interactive dashboards built with the team's chosen visualisation tool.

3

A Presentation Capturing Trends, Gaps & Opportunities

A concise 10-slide presentation with clear findings and storytelling.

4

Practical Recommendations for Akwa Ibom's Tourism Future

Data-backed strategic recommendations for decision-makers and stakeholders.
